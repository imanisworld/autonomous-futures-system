## Why the 2026-09-07 proof is retired

The 2026-09-08 forensic audit reproduced the proof exactly (63 arms, 57 fills,
+$1,026.64) and found two independent defects in it:

1. **Fill reference one 5-minute bar late.** Journal `bar_ts` is the 15-minute
   decision candle's *open* (verified against `data/replay_polygon/MNQ`); the
   candle completes at `bar_ts + 15m`. Every one of the 57 fills used the open
   of the 5m bar stamped `bar_ts + 20m` — the same arrival-bar look-ahead
   class the VWAP Hold reconciliation rejected on 2026-09-07.
2. **Fills held beyond their own bracket.** 37 of 57 fills landed past the
   mirrored stop (the breakout close is a median 68–76 ticks beyond the ORB
   level; the mirrored stop is 50 ticks). Those 37 carried +$1,087.74, more
   than the whole result, each exiting `STOP_HIT` in profit and labelled
   `LOSS`. PaperBroker now refuses such fills on every entry path (#508).

The earlier n=111 study (#364, +$745.72 / PF 2.39) has the same geometry
defect: its raw rows survive at commit `42a8618` on tag
`archive/codex-orb-breakout-marketable-limit-inverse-frozen-2026-07-27`
(`scripts/orb_breakout_marketable_limit_inverse_fixed_raw.jsonl`); **66 of
111 fills are bracket-invalid (+$837.32); the 45 valid fills net −$91.60,
PF 0.83.** Its own attribution table assigned +$819.60 of +$840.10 to "fill
selection". Retired on the same grounds.
