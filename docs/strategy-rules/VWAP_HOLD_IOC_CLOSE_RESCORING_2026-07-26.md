# VWAP Hold — IOC Reference-Price Re-Scoring (2026-07-26, amended same day)

Research/evidence re-scoring only. No production, config, strategy, broker,
Pine, or `risk_rules.yaml` changes. `vwap_hold` stays `SHADOW_ONLY` (with the
existing narrow MNQ proof-lane exception) — this document does not authorize
any promotion or deployment change.

**Amendment notice**: this document was revised after operator review of the
first version (PR #345). The first version's headline evidence was the
blended 348-arm population (london+new_york+asian). The strategy being
classified is **"VWAP Hold — MNQ NY"** — only New York-session signals were
ever live-eligible under current config (`risk_rules.yaml`'s global
`allowed_sessions: [new_york]` gate applies to every strategy live). The
blended population is only 30.7% New York (107/348) — the remaining 69.3%
(london+asian) could never have been live-eligible. **This amendment adds
the NY-only canonical evidence and demotes the blended-348 result to
robustness/provenance context.** The IOC reference-price decision itself
(§1) is unchanged.

## 4. `partial_2ct_approx` caveat (unchanged from the original version)

`partial_2ct_approx` is numerically strong in both the blended and NY-only
views, but it is **not a live-executable single-order model today** — it is
the tranche-1-documented approximation (`static_pnl(1ct) + runner_pnl(1ct)`,
two independent single-contract simulations summed), not a genuine
2-contract scale-out order. Per this repo's own tracked gap (`EXIT_MODE`
scaling support is built but INERT), this number should be read as an
**upper-bound approximation**, not an executable baseline, until
partial-fill-aware scale-out resolution exists.

**Recommendation: `vwap_hold` verdict is `PROMISING BUT UNPROVEN`** — this
was PR #345's original recommendation and it holds up under the corrected,
NY-only canonical population, per the operator's own provisional framing.
It is **not** upgraded further despite the NY-only PFs being numerically
higher than the blended ones (PF 2.18-3.37 NY-only vs 1.63-2.24 blended) —
that would be exactly backwards: a smaller, thinner sample producing a
higher point-estimate PF is a sign of small-sample noise/variance, not a
stronger edge, and the concentration figures above confirm this directly
(runner's headline PF is substantially carried by 3-5 outsized trades out of
55 total fills).

**Two caveats must be read as first-order, not footnotes:**
1. **The actual live-relevant execution sample is ~55 fills** (not 146, not
   348). Every dollar figure and ratio in §3 rests on this sample size.
   This is thin. It clears the taxonomy's literal ≥30 minimum but should not
   be read as a settled, high-confidence result.
2. **Winner concentration, especially under runner exit, is materially
   worse than the blended-348 headline suggested** — 71.2% of net P&L from
   5 of 31 winners at 2-tick under runner, worse than the already-flagged
   60M 3-2-2 precedent (54% from 5/18). This does not by itself disqualify
   PROMISING BUT UNPROVEN (the taxonomy's own definition is "positive
   replay evidence, not yet fully validated" — concentration risk is
   exactly the kind of thing that keeps a strategy at this tier rather than
   VALIDATED), but it is a real, not cosmetic, robustness concern that
   should weigh against any future upgrade until a larger NY-only sample
   either confirms or dilutes it.

**This does NOT clear VALIDATED.** Beyond the pre-existing Rules/Detector/
Replay-parity gaps (unchanged, out of scope), the ~55-fill sample and the
concentration findings above are themselves disqualifying for VALIDATED on
their own terms — "adequate sample" and "no unacceptable winner
concentration" are not comfortably met, only marginally/conditionally met.

## 7. What this task does NOT decide

**The exit-mode question — static vs runner vs partial — remains separately
unresolved and is explicitly NOT decided by this task or this amendment.**
All three exit modes are reported above for both the NY-only canonical
population and the blended-348 context population, per the operator's own
instruction not to pick a favorite. All three NY-only cells pass
both-halves-positive at every cost tier, which is encouraging, but — per
the operator's exact words — "does not authorize choosing runner/static/
partial here." Runner has the highest raw PF but also by far the worst
concentration profile of the three; static has the cleanest concentration
profile but the lowest PF; partial is not independently executable. Which
of these tradeoffs the operator wants to accept is a separate decision this
document does not make.

Also not decided or changed by this task: whether/when to promote
`vwap_hold` beyond `SHADOW_ONLY`; the `vwap_rejection` Pine-deployment
sequencing decision; the `vwap_reclaim` walk-forward-split question. All
three remain exactly as `VWAP_FAMILY_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_AUDIT_2026-07-26.md`
left them.
