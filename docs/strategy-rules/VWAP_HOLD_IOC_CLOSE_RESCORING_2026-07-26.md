Research/evidence re-scoring only. No production, config, strategy, broker,
Pine, or `risk_rules.yaml` changes. `vwap_hold` stays `SHADOW_ONLY` (with the
existing narrow MNQ proof-lane exception) — this document does not authorize
any promotion or deployment change.

## 1. Operator decision being implemented

> "VWAP Hold IOC reference price: choose `close` as canonical. Reason: that

that PR #307's isolated fill-model comparison checked IOC marketability
against the arrival bar's **open**
(`scripts/vwap_hold_paired_fill_comparison.py:158`), while every real
production/replay call site — `webhook/runner.py:2016-2018`,

`execution/paper_broker.py`'s own docstring (`:171-172`, `:213`) — uses
**close**. `ioc_open` is therefore **superseded/deprecated** as the
evidence-of-record for `vwap_hold`'s IOC leg; `ioc_close` is now the
canonical IOC reference. `ioc_open`'s numbers are retained in

### Chronological-halves detail (2-tick baseline; H1 = first 73 resolved, H2 = last 73)

| Exit | H1 exp/fill | H2 exp/fill |
|---|---:|---:|
| static | $11.43 | $1.74 |

**Both halves are positive at every cost tier for every exit mode — the
walk-forward gate is cleared** — but H2 is materially weaker than H1 in all
three exit modes (roughly 6-10x smaller expectancy/fill). This is a real
pattern, not noise, and should be read as "durable but decaying," not
"uniformly strong." It echoes the same H1>>H2 shape flagged elsewhere in
this repo's evidence (Corpus v1's `orb_reclaim`).

### `partial_2ct_approx` caveat

`partial_2ct_approx` is the strongest cell numerically at every cost tier,
but it is **not a live-executable single-order model today** — it is the
tranche-1-documented approximation (`static_pnl(1ct) + runner_pnl(1ct)`,
i.e. two independent single-contract simulations summed), not a genuine
2-contract scale-out order. Per this repo's own tracked gap
(`EXIT_MODE` scaling support is built but INERT — see the scaling-roadmap
thread), this number should be read as an **upper-bound approximation**, not
an executable baseline, until partial-fill-aware scale-out resolution
exists. It is reported here for completeness (per the instruction to report
all three exit modes), not recommended as the basis for any classification
upgrade on its own.

**Static exit's top-5 winners (2-tick)**: 4 of the 5 are from the single
first day in the dataset (2024-07-02, London session, entries at 07:00,
08:15, 11:00, 11:45, 12:45 UTC) — a genuine same-day clustering concern,
not just an aggregate-percentage concern. **Runner exit's top-5 (2-tick)**
is more diversified: 3 distinct dates (2025-03-27, 2024-09-03, 2025-02-27,
all New York) plus 2 from the same first day. **Partial's concentration is
the lowest of the three** (42.9% at 2-tick) because it inherits static's
more numerous smaller fills alongside runner's larger ones, diluting any
single trade's share — but this benefit is entirely a byproduct of the
non-executable approximation noted above, not a real diversification
mechanism.

Relative to sample size, this is a less extreme concentration profile than
the 60M 3-2-2 lane's flagged 54% top-5/18-winners (28% of winners) —
`vwap_hold`/`ioc_close`/runner's 49.3% top-5 comes from 5 of 75 winners
(6.7% of winners), a smaller fraction of a much larger winner pool. Still,
**41-59% of net P&L riding on 5 trades out of 146-148 resolved is a real
concentration risk**, not a clean, broadly-distributed edge, and should be
weighed as such.

## 5. Drawdown relative to expectancy

| Exit | Cost | Max DD | DD ÷ exp/fill | DD ÷ net P&L |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| static | 2t | $169.42 | 25.75x | 17.6% |
| runner | 2t | $187.78 | 18.93x | 13.0% |
| partial | 2t | $327.12 | 17.46x | 12.0% |

The DD÷exp/fill ratio is not directly comparable to smaller-n lanes (e.g.
60M 3-2-2's 2.1x on n=34) — with 146 resolved fills, per-fill expectancy is
naturally smaller relative to a drawdown built from a losing streak across
a much larger trade count, so a higher ratio here does not by itself
indicate worse risk control. The DD÷net-P&L view (12-18% of total realized
profit) is the more comparable framing across sample sizes and shows
**drawdown is a modest fraction of total profit in all three exit modes** —
controlled, not alarming, under any of the three.

**Recommendation: upgrade `vwap_hold`'s verdict from `WAIT — isolated fill
test pending` to `PROMISING BUT UNPROVEN`.**

Reasoning: the specific blocker this task was authorized to resolve — the
IOC reference-price question — is now resolved by operator decision, and
the resulting `ioc_close` evidence is substantially stronger than the
`ioc_open` evidence it supersedes: static exit flips from failing
both-halves at every cost tier to passing at every cost tier; runner and
partial were already passing and get materially better (runner: $2.69→$4.16
per armed at 2-tick, a +55% improvement; the fill rate itself improves from
30.2%→42.0%). Combined with `market`+runner's already-established $10.30/armed
(unaffected by this task, still the single strongest cell in the full
matrix), `vwap_hold` now has **multiple independently-passing entry×exit
combinations under honest, production-matching fill assumptions, adequate
sample, walk-forward survival, and slippage survival** — this is squarely
"positive replay evidence, not yet fully validated," the taxonomy's own
definition of PROMISING BUT UNPROVEN, and is a materially different
evidentiary position than WAIT ("rules incomplete or detector missing" — the
detector is not missing, and this task adds no new rules-completeness
information either way).

**This does NOT clear VALIDATED.** VALIDATED requires passing all eight
dimensions, and Rules/Detector/Replay-parity remain at their pre-task master
table classification (unresolved by this task, deliberately not
re-litigated here — that is the separate, already-flagged audit thread).
There is also no live/paper-trading evidence yet (`vwap_hold` remains
`SHADOW_ONLY` except the narrow MNQ proof-lane), and the exit-mode question
(§7) is unresolved, meaning there is no single canonical cell to point to as
"the" validated baseline — three different cells, three different numbers,
all positive but not unified into one evaluated strategy configuration.

**If forced to name a single strongest fully-honest-fill cell**: `market` +
`runner` remains the headline ($10.30/armed, 2-tick, PF 1.52, n=343/348
filled) — this was already true before this task and is not changed by it.
Among the three `ioc_close` cells, `runner` is the strongest non-approximated
one ($4.16/armed, PF 2.14, both halves positive at every tier, most
diversified top-5 concentration of the three). `static` is now viable for
the first time under `ioc_close` (previously it was harmful under every
tested entry model) but remains the weakest of the three `ioc_close` cells.
`partial_2ct_approx` is numerically best but rests on a non-executable
approximation (§3) and should not be treated as a real baseline pending
scale-out order support.
