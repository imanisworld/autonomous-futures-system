## Reading

- n=3 / n=1. Nothing here can confirm or reject the in-sample +$120 (16 trades) control or the
  +$522.50 fallback counterfactual. The window is too short by an order of magnitude.
- The preemption pattern repeats out of sample: 2 of 3 executable 1-2-2 candidates were lost to
  shadow-only `vwap_hold` winning the bar and then producing NO_TRADE — pure opportunity loss.
- Both out-of-sample preemptions fail at the **permission gate** (`STRATEGY_NOT_PAPER_ELIGIBLE`),
  not at `ENTRY_DETACHED_FROM_PRICE`. The candidate-loop fallback studied on 2026-09-08 only
  recovers ENTRY_DETACHED cases, so that lever would have recovered **0 of 2** here. The
  permission-gate location, which the 09-08 study set aside, is the one that would have fired.

## Caveats

- `mes_122_fallback_full_engine_proof._run()` reads each day's journal immediately after that day
  replays, so an OUTCOME written on a later day is missed and the trade is classified
  `TRADE_UNRESOLVED`. The 08-18 trade hit that path. This runner re-reads the final journals
  after both passes (`refresh_outcomes`). The proof script itself was not changed; whether any
  in-sample row is affected has not been checked.
- This run was executed from a worktree with no `.env`. Diffing `load_config()` with and without
  the checkout `.env` shows 12 differing fields, all non-engine (Discord, GEX observe, Signa,
  options companion, log_dir, key-configured flags). None feed the replay decision path, and the
  frozen #373 override sets every decision-relevant field explicitly.
