## Adapter contract and timestamp (repo `7fdc9252`)

`research/databento_322_bar_adapter.py` does not pick a continuous contract. It calls `sources.polygon_client.front_contract` with `DEFAULT_ROLL_DAYS = 8`, keeps only quarterly outrights, and timestamps bars as UTC bar-start instants. Front-month is keyed on the civil ET date of that start.

- `front_contract("MNQ", date(2026, 3, 6))` → **MNQH6**
- Third Friday of March 2026 = 2026-03-20
- Roll window starts `2026-03-20 − 8 days` = **2026-03-12** (first day the front becomes MNQM6)
- 15:00 UTC = 10:00 EST on 2026-03-06, so the civil ET date is still 2026-03-06
- Definitions: MNQH6 is `instrument_id` **42004946**, maturity 2026-03, expiration 2026-03-20T13:30:00+00:00
- `_select_bars` keeps only that `instrument_id`. `_aggregate` takes first open, max high, min low, last close, summed volume.

That is the same rule the committed 5-minute tree used. It is not a wrong-contract pick and not an end-of-bar timestamp shift.
