# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Repository discussions must remain respectful, factual, and focused on the
public educational project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam,
financial-performance claims, and attempts to solicit credentials or private
strategy material are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to public issues, security reports, and other interactions related
to this repository.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when necessary. Report sensitive security concerns privately
through GitHub's vulnerability-reporting feature, not in a public issue.
