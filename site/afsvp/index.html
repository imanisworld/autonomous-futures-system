Phase 1
Current posture: advisory and evidence collection only

No public brokerage, investment-advisory, trade-execution, or order-submission service is offered through this site.

What this is

AFSVP is private research and operator infrastructure for testing market-alert quality, paper/demo behavior, strategy evidence, and system reliability before any broader capability is trusted.

What this is not

This site does not provide recommendations, accept trades, expose execution controls, offer brokerage access, or make any claim that a strategy is profitable or approved for live use.
